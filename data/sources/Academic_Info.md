## D) Other Fees

- The administration fee for the undergraduate program is $200 per year, while the graduate program is $250 per year (subject to annual revision).
- This fee covers year-round access to library services, lab services, start-up rooms, conferences, a student ID card, and supervision/mentorship from a faculty member.
- Students are required to have a personal computer for self-study and other activities.
- Students are responsible for other personal expenses such as study materials and other expenses.

## VI. Admission Criteria

To be admitted into the programs at CamTech, applicants must meet the following requirements:

## A) Bachelor's Degree

- High school diploma or an equivalent certificate (original/ certified copy)
- A valid minimum of English Proficiency (IELTS = 5.5, TOEFL iBT = 68, TOEFL PBT = 512, TOEFL CBT = 84, TOEIC = 570, or CEFR = B2)
- One of these documents: birth certificate, ID card, or a valid passport
- Current photo of 4 x 6 (with a white background)
- Passing the CamTech entrance exam and interview

## B) Master's Degree

- Bachelor's degree or an equivalent certificate (original/ certified copy)
- Transcript of a bachelor's degree (original/ certified copy)
- A valid minimum of English Proficiency (IELTS = 6.0, TOEFL iBT = 79, TOEFL PBT = 540, TOEFL CBT = 207, TOEIC = 620, or CEFR = C1)
- One of the following documents: birth certificate, ID card or valid passport
- A letter of recommendation from the workplace (for research stream)
- A letter of motivation and an updated CV
- An initial research proposal (for research stream)
- Current photo of 4 x 6 (with a white background)
- Passing the CamTech interview

## C)  Doctoral Degree

- An original/ certified copy of m aster's degree (preferably by research) in science, technology, political science, economics, development studies, education, social science, or a related field
- Transcript of a master's degree with a minimum GPA of 3.50 from an accredited educational institution in Cambodia or overseas (original/ certified copy)

- Have at least two years of working experience in a related field and be approved by CamTech's Admission Committee
- Have a strong research proposal that can be turned into a doctoral dissertation; the proposal should be practically relevant to the industry
- Have a proven track record of publications prior to applying
- Passing the CamTech interview, or being accepted by a CamTech doctoral supervisor who will be designated as the student's principal supervisor (Please see Appendix A for potential supervisors)
- Must have at least an industrial mentor with at least a master's degree regardless of the sector the applicant is working in
- Two letters of recommendation. At least one of them must come from the potential industrial mentor at the student's workplace or prospective employer agreeing to supervise the student's doctoral dissertation
- A valid minimum of English Proficiency (IELTS = 6.5, TOEFL iBT = 80, TOEFL PBT = 550, TOEFL CBT = 214, TOEIC = 690, or CEFR = C1)
- One of the following documents: birth certificate, ID card, or a valid passport
- A letter of motivation and an updated CV
- Current photo of 4 x 6 (with a white background)

* If an applicant does not have a valid English Proficiency certificate, he/she must take an English placement test at CamTech. However, the test may be waived if:

- The applicant is a native English speaker.
- The applicant has attended a school where all subjects were taught in English and the school is well-recognized.
- The applicant has a certificate of English proficiency from a recognized school, which CamTech can evaluate and accept.

## VII. How to Apply

To apply to any program, applicants must follow the following steps:

1. Click online application form (undergraduate program) https://camtech.edu.kh/apply, or https://ee-eu.kobotoolbox.org/x/FaebcANX (graduate program).
2. Fill out the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Upload the required documents (school diploma, transcript, English proficiency certificate, and other supporting materials)
4. Pay the application fee through the QR code provided in the online application form.
