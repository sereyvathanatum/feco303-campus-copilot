# CamTech Campus Handbook 2026-2027

Demo edition for the Campus Copilot learning pack.

Illustrative text for a demo system; not official CamTech policy.

This handbook sets out the rules and services of a demo campus. The rules apply to every enrolled account unless a section states otherwise. Sections are numbered, and each section starts on its own page so that citations can point to a page.

Questions about a rule go to the office named in the section. The contacts directory is on the last English page.

# 1. Attendance at lectures

Attendance is recorded at every lecture by an ID-card scan during the first 15 minutes. Arrival more than 15 minutes after the start counts as late, and three late arrivals count as one absence.

Lecture attendance below 80 percent of the scheduled lectures in a course removes eligibility for the final exam of that course, unless the Registrar approves an exception.

An absence covered by a medical certificate is recorded as excused when the certificate reaches the Registrar within five working days of the absence. Excused absences do not count towards the 80 percent threshold.

Attendance records can be checked on the campus portal at any time. A disputed record is raised with the course coordinator within one week.

<!-- page -->
# 2. Attendance at lab sessions

Lab sessions carry hands-on assessment and are not repeated outside the published timetable.

More than three missed lab sessions in one course, excused absences not counted, set the lab component of the course grade to zero and trigger a meeting with the program coordi-
nator.

A missed lab can be made up once per course by attending another section of the same course in the same week, with written approval from the course coordinator before the session.

A lab report for a missed session is still due at the published deadline. Lab attendance is recorded by an ID-card scan at the lab door.

# 3. Grading scale

Course grades use the following scale:

- A: 85 to 100 marks (grade point 4.0)
- B+: 80 to 84 marks (grade point 3.5)
- B: 70 to 79 marks (grade point 3.0)
- C+: 65 to 69 marks (grade point 2.5)
- C: 50 to 64 marks (grade point 2.0)
- F: below 50 marks (grade point 0.0)

The pass mark for a course is 50. A course graded F is repeated in a later semester. Grades are published on the campus portal within 15 working days after the final exam.

A grade review request goes to the course coordinator within ten working days of publication. A review can raise or lower the grade.

# 4. Late assignments

Late assignments lose 10 percent of the available marks for each calendar day after the deadline, for up to three days. Work submitted more than three days late receives zero marks.

Deadlines fall at 23:59 Phnom Penh time on the published date. The submission timestamp recorded by the learning platform is final.

Group work counts as late when the last required file arrives late. The penalty applies to every member of the group.

An approved extension (section 5) removes the late penalty until the new deadline. Technical problems with a personal device are not grounds for removing a penalty; the learning platform accepts early submissions and resubmissions until the deadline.

<!-- page -->
# 5. Extensions

An extension request goes to the course coordinator before the deadline, with supporting evidence such as a medical certificate or an official letter.

An approved extension lasts at most seven calendar days. A request made after the deadline is accepted only for a medical emergency supported by a certificate.

Extensions are not available for exams, in-class quizzes, or lab sessions. Missed exams follow section 6.

<!-- page -->
# 6. Examinations

Each course has two written exams: the midterm, which is the first exam, held in week 8 of the semester, and the final, which is the second exam, held in the last week of the semester. The midterm carries 25 percent and the final 35 percent of the course grade, unless the course specification states other weights.

Entry to an exam room requires a valid campus ID card. Phones are switched off and kept in bags at the front of the room. Entry closes 30 minutes after the start of the exam.

A missed midterm with a medical certificate is replaced by a make-up exam within two weeks. A missed final with a medical certificate leads to a deferred exam in the next exam period. A missed exam without a certificate scores zero.

# 7. Academic integrity

Submitted work must be the work of the account that submits it, with every source acknowledged. Plagiarism, contract cheating, unauthorised collaboration, and fabricated data are breaches of academic integrity.

Penalties rise with each confirmed case:

- first case: zero marks for the work and a written warning;
- second case: an F grade for the course;
- third case: referral to the disciplinary board, which can suspend enrolment.

Similarity reports from the learning platform support a decision but never decide a case on their own. A case is heard by the course coordinator and one independent member of staff.

<!-- page -->
# 8. Use of AI tools in coursework

Each course specification states whether generative AI tools are allowed for an assignment. When a course allows them, the submission includes a short disclosure statement that names the tool, the purpose, and the parts of the work it touched.

Undisclosed use of an AI tool, where disclosure is required, is treated as a breach of academic integrity under section 7.

AI tools are not allowed in written exams. Personal data of other people and confidential campus records are never pasted into external AI services.

# 9. Library loans and fines

Up to five books can be on loan to one account at a time. The standard loan period is 14 days. Reference books and theses are for use inside the library only.

A loan can be renewed online twice, provided no hold has been placed on the book.

Overdue books incur a fine of 500 riel per book per day, capped at 20,000 riel per book. Borrowing is suspended while any fine is unpaid.

A lost or damaged book is charged at its replacement cost plus a processing fee of 10,000 riel.

# 10. Library holds and opening hours

A hold can be placed on a book when no copy is available. A returned book with a hold waits at the loans desk for three days before it passes to the next hold. Each account can hold at most three books at a time.

Opening hours during the semester:

- Monday to Friday: 07:30 to 20:00
- Saturday: 08:00 to 12:00
- Sunday and public holidays: closed

The second floor is a quiet floor. Group study rooms inside the library are booked at the loans desk for up to two hours.

<!-- page -->
# 11. Rooms, labs, and bookings

Classrooms and study rooms not used by a scheduled session can be booked through the campus system for up to two hours per booking and up to seven days ahead. Each account can hold at most three active bookings.

A booking not claimed within 15 minutes of its start time is released. Bookings are cancelled through the same system, and a cancellation frees the room at once.

Computer labs C-301 and C-302 require a completed lab induction before use. Food and drink are not allowed in computer labs.

Projector remotes and cables are collected from the building desk against a campus ID card and returned the same day.

<!-- page -->
# 12. Wi-Fi and IT accounts

The campus network "CamTech-Secure" uses the campus account name and password. The "CamTech-Guest" network gives visitors two hours of access per day.

Up to three devices can be connected per account at the same time.

Passwords expire every 180 days. A forgotten password is reset on the IT portal with the recovery e-mail address, or in person at the IT Helpdesk with a campus ID card.

Suspected phishing messages are forwarded to the IT Helpdesk and then deleted. The IT Helpdesk never asks for a password by e-mail or phone.

<!-- page -->
# 13. Campus ID cards

A campus ID card is issued at enrolment and is carried at all times on campus. The card opens buildings, labs, and the library, and it is required at exams.

A lost card is reported to Security at once so that its access can be blocked. A replacement card costs 5 US dollars, paid at the Fees Office, and is ready within two working days.

A damaged card is replaced free of charge when the old card is handed in.

# 14. Fees and payments

Tuition fees are paid per semester in two instalments: the first by the end of week 2 and the second by the end of week 9. A late payment adds a fee of 2 percent of the overdue amount per month.

Payment is made by bank transfer to the campus account printed on the invoice, or in cash at the Fees Office counter. Every payment produces an electronic receipt.

Payment disputes, such as a fee charged twice or a missing receipt, are handled by the Fees Office: fees@camtech.example.edu, extension 214, Monday to Friday 08:00 to 11:30 and 13:30 to 16:30. Refunds of confirmed double payments are made within ten working days.

# 15. Campus services

The cafeteria on the ground floor of Building A opens from 07:00 to 19:00 on weekdays and from 07:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays.

The health room next to the Security desk in Building A offers first aid during opening hours. Counselling appointments are booked at the Services Desk and are confidential.

Printing costs 200 riel per black-and-white page and 1,000 riel per colour page, paid with credit on the campus ID card.

Lost property is kept at the Security desk for 30 days.

<!-- page -->
# 16. Safety and emergencies

In an emergency, campus Security answers on extension 999 at any hour. Fire alarms mean immediate evacuation by the nearest stairway to the assembly point in the main courtyard. Lifts are not used during an evacuation.

Every incident, including a near miss in a lab, is reported to Security on the same day. Lab safety rules posted at each lab door apply in addition to this handbook.
