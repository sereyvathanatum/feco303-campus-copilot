# Campus services FAQ

Illustrative text for a demo system; not official CamTech policy.

## Wi-Fi and IT

### How is a device connected to the campus Wi-Fi?

Select the network "CamTech-Secure" and sign in with the campus account name and password. Visitors use "CamTech-Guest", which allows two hours per day.

### What happens after a forgotten password?

The password is reset on the IT portal with the recovery e-mail address, or at the IT Helpdesk in Building C with a campus ID card.

## Printing

### Where can documents be printed?

Printers stand on every floor of the library and in Building C. Printing is paid with credit on the campus ID card; credit is topped up at the Services Desk.

## Room bookings

### How is a study room booked?

Rooms are booked in the campus system or through the Campus Copilot. A booking lasts up to two hours and can be made up to seven days ahead. The booking is released when nobody claims it within 15 minutes of the start.

## Library

### How is a loan renewed?

Loans are renewed on the library page of the campus portal, twice per loan, unless another account has placed a hold on the book.

## ID cards

### Where is a replacement ID card requested?

A replacement is requested at the Registrar after the fee is paid at the Fees Office. The new card is ready within two working days.

## Counselling and health

### How is a counselling appointment made?

Appointments are booked at the Services Desk in Building A or by e-mail to services@camtech.example.edu. Appointments are confidential.
